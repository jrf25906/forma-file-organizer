# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-02-04

This Privacy Policy describes how **Forma** (“Forma”, “we”, “us”) handles
information when you use the Forma macOS application (the “App”) and our
websites (the “Site”).

## Summary

- **Local-first:** Forma is designed so your files and file data stay on your Mac.
- **No file uploads:** We do not upload your file names, folder structure, or file
  contents to our servers for processing.

## Information the App processes and stores on your device

To provide file-organization features, the App may read and process file
metadata and store related data locally on your device, such as:

- File metadata (for example: file name, path, size, timestamps, and type)
- Rules, templates, preferences, and feature-flag settings
- Activity history and undo history
- On-device analytics/insights derived from your files
- Optional on-device “smart” features (for example: clustering or pattern
  detection) and their locally stored outputs

This information is stored on your device (e.g., in local app storage and/or a
local database). It is not transmitted to us as part of normal App operation.

## Security-scoped bookmarks and permissions

On macOS, the App can only access folders you explicitly grant access to. When
you grant folder access, the App may store a **security-scoped bookmark** so it
can continue to access that folder in the future without repeatedly prompting
you. Bookmarks are stored locally (and may be stored in the macOS Keychain).

## Information the App does not collect

We do not run third-party analytics SDKs in the App, and we do not sell, rent,
or share App data for advertising.

If you choose to contact support and share logs, screenshots, or files, that
information is provided by you and used only to help troubleshoot or respond.

## Website data

When you visit the Site, we may receive standard web request data (for example:
IP address, user agent, and approximate timestamp) through hosting logs. We may
use cookies strictly necessary for site functionality. If we add analytics on
the Site, we will update this policy and describe what is collected and how to
opt out where applicable.

## Data sharing

We do not share App data with third parties. The App is distributed via the Mac
App Store, and purchases (if any) are processed by Apple. Apple’s handling of
information is governed by Apple’s own policies.

## Data retention

Data stored by the App remains on your device until you delete it (for example,
by deleting the App, clearing its data, or using in-app reset options where
available).

## Security

Forma is built with macOS sandboxing and uses system security mechanisms (such
as security-scoped bookmarks and the Keychain) to help protect your data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we’ll
update the effective date above.
